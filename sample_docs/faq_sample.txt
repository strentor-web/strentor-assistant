STRENTOR Frequently Asked Questions (sample)

Who is the free Assessment for?
The STRENTOR Assessment is for anyone who wants to understand their starting point. It takes a few minutes, needs no payment, and recommends a coaching pathway.

How long is the Strategy Session?
The Strategy Session lasts 60 minutes. It reviews your current training, assesses risk and load, gives three specific changes, and ends with a written summary.

What equipment is needed?
Most sessions use resistance bands, light dumbbells, and a sturdy chair or wheelchair. No gym membership is needed.

How is the Flagship Founding Cohort structured?
The Founding Cohort runs for twelve weeks. It combines strength sessions, nutrition guidance, mindset check-ins, and weekly accountability messages with the coach.

Is coaching a replacement for medical care?
No. Coaching complements medical care. Participants should follow guidance from their doctor or therapist, and the coach adapts training around it.

How are results tracked?
Progress is tracked with a simple log of strength numbers, energy levels, and weekly reflections. Outcomes vary from person to person and results are not guaranteed.
