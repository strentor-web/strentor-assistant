# STRENTOR (public overview, from strentor.com)

STRENTOR is a premium, founder-led, wheelchair-first strength, mindset and purpose coaching brand. It coaches ambitious wheelchair users in India, the UAE, Singapore and selected international markets. The founder is Aditya Mandan, a wheelchair user born with Spina Bifida Myelomeningocele, a certified fitness trainer and nutrition consultant, and a national-level para powerlifter. Tagline: Break. Build. Become.

## Who it is for
Ambitious wheelchair users who want structured progress in strength, confidence and direction, and who are willing to be coached, tracked and held accountable. STRENTOR does not define clients primarily by diagnosis.

## The offers
STRENTOR sells one primary coaching programme, STRENTOR Flagship. The other offers are an entry point, a one-off session, a membership and a corporate service.
- STRENTOR Assessment: free, takes a few minutes. It reviews your goals, current capability and constraints, and recommends a pathway. Start at /strentor-assessment.
- Strategy Session: a one-hour session (priced at 4,999 rupees in India) with a coach who trains from a chair himself. It includes a review of your current training, a direct assessment of risk and load, three specific changes to make, and a written summary afterwards. It is not an ongoing programme.
- STRENTOR Flagship: Founding Cohort: the primary programme. It runs 12 weeks and is founder-led with Aditya Mandan. It has two delivery modes: Trainer-Led (live 1:1 coaching sessions) and Self-Paced (asynchronous coaching). It is a one-time 12-week pack, not a monthly fee. You choose 3, 4 or 5 sessions per week (3 is the minimum). It is not suitable if you cannot commit to twelve weeks.
- STRENTOR Circle: an optional membership after the programme ends, with ongoing accountability, a peer community, an adaptive workout library, monthly group sessions, and habit and progress tracking. It is half price for anyone on a STRENTOR coaching programme. It is not a starting point.
- Corporate and Institutional programmes: wheelchair-first inclusion workshops and programmes for organisations, NGOs and CSR partners. Pricing is scoped with STRENTOR, so contact the team to discuss it.
The old 7-Day Starter Kit and the Elite tier are no longer sold.

## Flagship Trainer-Led includes
Personalised live 1:1 coaching sessions on training days (up to 5 per week, depending on what suits you), individually designed adaptive strength programming, personalised nutrition guidance within coaching scope, regular progress assessment and tracking, form and technique feedback with programme adjustments, accountability and coaching support, and direct founder-led coaching from Aditya Mandan.

## Flagship Self-Paced includes
A personalised adaptive training programme built around your capabilities, goals, equipment and schedule, a weekly structured asynchronous check-in, an initial video form review for every prescribed exercise, follow-up form reviews when an exercise changes or technique needs reassessment, programme adjustments based on your progress, and messaging support with replies within one working day.

## Pricing
Flagship is priced per session. In India the rate is 1,000 rupees per session for Trainer-Led and 500 rupees per session for Self-Paced. Every other country gets a computed regional price, shown on the Programs, Coaching and Pricing page of strentor.com, which has a visible country selector. Founding members receive preferential founding pricing while STRENTOR builds its first cohort.

## Coaching approach
Four connected areas: Strength, Nutrition, Mindset and Purpose, delivered through the STRENTOR Method: Break, Build, Become. The process is personal assessment, adaptive programming, coaching feedback, accountability, progress tracking and ongoing refinement.

## Getting started
Take the free STRENTOR Assessment, or the Find Your Starting Point questionnaire, on strentor.com. They need no payment or commitment. A Readiness Checklist and a Self-Reflection Quiz are also available.

## Important notes
STRENTOR coaching is not medical or psychological treatment and complements medical care. Outcomes vary and results are not guaranteed.

## Frequently asked questions

Is STRENTOR physiotherapy or rehabilitation?
No. STRENTOR is fitness and performance coaching, not medical treatment, not physiotherapy, and not a substitute for care from a doctor, physiotherapist, or dietitian. Coaching is designed to work alongside your existing medical care, not in place of it.

Who is STRENTOR designed for?
Ambitious wheelchair users focused on building strength, confidence, identity, and direction. STRENTOR does not define clients primarily by diagnosis. It is built for people who want structured progress and are willing to be coached, tracked, and held accountable.

Do I need gym equipment?
It depends on your plan and goals. Many clients start with minimal equipment: resistance bands, light dumbbells, or bodyweight work adapted to a seated position. What you actually have access to is confirmed during your assessment, not assumed upfront.

Can I train from home?
Yes. Coaching is delivered virtually, with video-guided sessions, direct form feedback, and structured check-ins, so training fits into your existing space and schedule.

Is coaching available outside India?
Yes. STRENTOR coaches clients across India, the UAE, Singapore, and selected international markets, with pricing and scheduling adapted to your region.

How personalized is the program?
Every coaching plan is built around your current strength and movement capacity, the wheelchair and equipment you use, your training environment, daily routine, ambitions, and relevant health guidance, not a standing-body template with exercises swapped out.

How are health considerations handled?
Your health context is discussed directly during your assessment, and programming is built around it. Where a condition requires medical clearance or ongoing monitoring, that stays with your treating doctor. Coaching is designed to work alongside it, not replace it.

Does STRENTOR provide medical or psychological treatment?
No. STRENTOR does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or psychological condition. Mental and emotional coaching support performance, consistency, and confidence, and are not a substitute for licensed mental-health treatment.

What commitment is expected?
Consistent participation. STRENTOR is built for clients willing to follow a structured plan, track progress, and be coached and held accountable. Meaningful transformation depends on consistency, not a single session.

What is the STRENTOR Method?
The STRENTOR Method is the framework every STRENTOR program is delivered through: Break, Build, Become. Break identifies what has actually gotten in your way. Build puts a personalised training, nutrition, mindset and accountability system in place. Become consolidates progress into lasting habits, confidence and direction.

How do I know which STRENTOR program is right for me?
Start with a free STRENTOR Assessment. It takes a few minutes and STRENTOR will recommend the right pathway, usually STRENTOR Flagship, the one primary coaching programme, for a full 12-week Founding Cohort transformation. The full offer ladder is on the Programs, Coaching and Pricing page.

Do I have to join STRENTOR Circle after my program ends?
No. STRENTOR Circle is optional. Many clients continue into it to keep accountability and community going after STRENTOR Flagship, but it is not required.

How does the application process work?
You complete the coaching application. STRENTOR reviews your goals, readiness, and coaching fit. Qualified applicants are invited to a discovery conversation, the right coaching pathway is recommended, and onboarding with a baseline assessment begins from there.

Can a family member participate in the initial conversation?
Yes. Family members or caregivers are welcome to join the discovery conversation, which is especially common when a family member is helping evaluate or fund coaching for an adult wheelchair user.

Are results guaranteed?
No. Individual outcomes vary and depend on participation, starting condition, available equipment, medical considerations, and other personal factors. STRENTOR does not guarantee weight loss, strength gains, medical improvement, independence, or the cure or reversal of any condition.

How is personal and health-related information protected?
Information you share is used only to evaluate your application and build your coaching plan. The Privacy Policy on strentor.com gives full detail on what is collected and how it is handled.
