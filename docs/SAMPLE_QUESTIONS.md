## Website FAQ (built in, no loading needed)

The public knowledge base includes all 16 questions from the strentor.com FAQ page plus 11 questions about the current offers (Flagship, Strategy Session, Circle, Assessment, countries, corporate, and the retired Starter Kit) (physiotherapy, equipment, training from home, regions, personalisation, health considerations, commitment, the STRENTOR Method, choosing a programme, Circle, the application process, family involvement, guarantees, privacy). The app lists every sample question under **All sample questions** on the Chat tab (41 in total); click any one to ask it.



Note: corporate programmes have no fixed module list. They are scoped per organisation after requirements are discussed, and both the public knowledge base and the sample PDF say so (try "what are corporate modules").
